## Composition

Use native safe areas, graphic hierarchy and layout selectors. Keep captions clear of faces, titles and important illustrations. Preserve contrast at mobile size. Do not copy episode-specific art or text as a generic brand rule. The renderer and template are the authority for exact geometry.

## Signature Edition

Ivory, forest green and terracotta remain the series palette. Keep elevation profile compact, north indicator restrained, and final poster clear of social UI. Photo motion remains slow and text unchanged. Optional light effects must not obscure real geography or imply actual recorded motion.

## Hybrid footage

Maintain documentary typography, restrained forest and gold accents, safe areas and credits. Choose real footage for meaningful natural motion. Crop with a single deliberate focal point for the complete shot. Do not layer still-photo optical effects over moving footage. Keep readable titles and a quiet soundtrack. Source adaptations retain applicable license obligations.
