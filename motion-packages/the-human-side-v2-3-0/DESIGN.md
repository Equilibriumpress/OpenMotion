## Composition

Use native safe areas, graphic hierarchy and layout selectors. Keep captions clear of faces, titles and important illustrations. Preserve contrast at mobile size. Do not copy episode-specific art or text as a generic brand rule. The renderer and template are the authority for exact geometry.
